LS4SRec + user-adaptive popularity penalty
Variants Results Example

Amazon Home & Kitchen · same trained model · only the final ranking changes

The model stays. The ranking learns who likes popular items.

LS4SRec is trained once. E1 is that model’s original ranking. E3 subtracts a popularity penalty that is larger for users whose own history is less popular, and smaller for users who already buy bestsellers.

s′(u, v) = s(u, v) − βu · qv

qv is item popularity from training purchases only, scaled from 0 to 1 with a log. qu is the average qv of the items that user already bought.

What E1–E4 do

Every variant re-sorts the same scores. βmax was chosen on the validation set: the strongest long-tail Recall@10 that still keeps Recall@10 at least 95% of E1. Chosen values: E2 = 2.83, everything else = 7.07.

E1 · paper

Original LS4SRec

βu = 0

The ranking from the trained model. This is the baseline the extension is compared with.

E2

One penalty for every user

βu = βmax

Popular items are pushed down by the same amount no matter who is asking.

E3 · extension

Penalty follows the user’s taste

βu = βmax · (1 − qu)

A user who already buys popular items gets a weak penalty. A user whose history is niche gets a strong one.

E3-rank

Use the user’s percentile

βu = βmax · (1 − rank of qu)

Replaces the raw average with where that user sits among all users, from most niche to most popularity-loving.

E3-rank-shrink

Short histories shrink to the mean

(n·qu + 5·mean) / (n + 5), then rank

A user with few purchases is pulled toward the average taste before the percentile is taken. 65% of users have 5 or fewer training items.

E4

Taste of the last 3 items

mix = α·qu + (1−α)·qlast 3, then rank

The trained α is negative for every test user, so clipping α to 0–1 makes E4 use only the last three items.

E3 beats E2 on long-tail recall (significant). E3-rank, E3-rank-shrink, and E4 are not significantly better than E3 on long-tail recall. Their Recall@10 losses versus E1 are significant and stay inside the 5% validation budget. On validation the pre-set rule selected E4; on test that edge over E3 is not significant, and E3 keeps more accuracy, so E3 is the method shown here.
